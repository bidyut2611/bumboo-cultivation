India has over 130 species of bamboo, but finding the right commercial grade requires connecting with local growers over vast geographies.

  • 📍 Northeast Hub: Highest density of paper and pulp species (Melocanna baccifera).
  • 🏗️ Central India: Abundant Solid Bamboo (Dendrocalamus strictus) for construction.
  • 🎋 Southern States: Exceptional Giant Bamboo (Dendrocalamus asper) for structural use.
